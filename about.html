About B.D. Motshegwa & Co.

Trusted Investment Banking Partner in Botswana

Our Story

Founded with a vision to empower investors, entrepreneurs, youth and businesses across Botswana.

B.D. Motshegwa & Co. is a premier investment banking firm dedicated to providing financial advisory, capital raising, merger & acquisition services. We combine global expertise with local knowledge to help our clients achieve sustainable growth.

Our team has successfully closed over 50 transactions, ranging from startup funding rounds to multi-million pula corporate mergers.

Our Mission

To democratize access to investment banking expertise and help local businesses thrive in the global economy.

Our Vision

To become Botswana’s most trusted financial partner, driving economic growth through smart capital solutions and data-driven financial strategies.
